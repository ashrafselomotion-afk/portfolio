Questions

Before the first render.

What do you need to start a 3D project?

A brief, visual references, required formats and any available product models or CAD files are useful. If no 3D model exists, the modelling scope can be assessed from drawings or reference photography.

Can you create content for unusual LED screens?

Yes. I can design and deliver for ultra-wide, vertical, curved and multi-panel screens when the final pixel map, safe areas and playback specification are provided.

Do you combine CGI with filmed footage?

Yes. Tracking, keying, compositing, cleanup, color matching and motion graphics can be used to integrate 3D elements into live-action plates.

How is a 3D project quoted?

Scope depends on modelling complexity, duration, level of realism, animation, render resolution and revisions. A storyboard or shot list makes the estimate much more precise.
