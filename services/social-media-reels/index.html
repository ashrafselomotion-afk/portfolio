Questions

Before the first post.

Which social platforms do you edit for?

I create content for Instagram Reels, TikTok, YouTube Shorts, LinkedIn, Facebook and paid media placements, with each format adapted to its platform and audience.

Can you turn one shoot into several videos?

Yes. I can plan and edit a content set from one production day, including multiple hooks, short cutdowns, captioned versions and different aspect ratios.

Do you add subtitles and motion graphics?

Yes. Captions, graphic callouts, title animation, transitions, sound design and visual effects can all be included when they help the message.

Can you support monthly social content?

Yes. I can work on a retained content schedule with an agreed volume, delivery calendar and reusable visual system to keep production efficient.
