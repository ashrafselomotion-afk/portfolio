Questions

Before the first cut.

What footage and files do you need?

I typically need the source footage, brief, brand guidelines, logos, music direction and a clear list of required formats. If the footage is still being planned, I can help define a shot list.

Can you edit on site at an event?

Yes. I can support fast-turnaround and same-day edits on site in Dubai, Abu Dhabi or Sharjah when planned into the production schedule.

Do you provide color and sound?

Yes. Editing projects can include color correction, audio cleanup, sound design, music editing, motion graphics, captions and delivery mastering.

Can one film be adapted for several platforms?

Yes. I can create 16:9, 1:1, 4:5 and 9:16 versions, shorter cutdowns, captioned variants and clean masters from one approved campaign edit.
