What I create

Brand systems that know how to move.

Good motion design is more than adding movement. It uses timing, hierarchy and sound to make a message easier to understand and harder to forget. Every asset is designed around the brand, the audience and the screen it needs to live on.

Brand & logo animation

Logo reveals, idents, transitions and reusable motion principles that make a visual identity feel consistent across channels.

Kinetic typography

Type-led films, social announcements and campaign messages choreographed for clarity, rhythm and attention.

Campaign motion systems

Modular templates and animation toolkits for paid media, organic social, web, DOOH and event screens.

Explainers & infographics

Complex products, data and ideas translated into structured visual stories with animation, iconography and sound.

Questions

Before the first keyframe.

What does a motion graphics designer deliver?

Depending on the brief, delivery can include concept development, styleframes, storyboards, 2D or 3D animation, compositing, sound design and final masters for social, web, broadcast or event screens.

Can you work with an existing brand guideline?

Yes. I can extend an established identity into motion or help define practical motion principles when the brand does not yet have them.

Do you support Arabic and English content?

Yes. I work in Arabic and English and can prepare layouts, typography and exports for multilingual UAE campaigns.

Can you join an agency or in-house team?

Yes. I work project-to-project, on a freelance retainer or embedded with a creative team, remotely or on site where the schedule requires it.
